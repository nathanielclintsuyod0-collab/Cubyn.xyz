✦ THE LAWS OF THE REALM ✦

PLAY WITH HONOR.

Simple rules keep Seraphyx fun and fair for everyone.

01

Respect Players

No harassment, threats, targeted bullying or hateful behavior.

02

No Cheating

No hacked clients, unfair exploits, duping or unauthorized advantages.

03

Protect the Economy

No exploiting shops, trades or bugs to create unfair wealth.

04

Keep It Appropriate

Keep chat, builds and usernames suitable for a mixed-age community.

05

Follow Staff Directions

Staff decisions and appeals should be handled calmly through the proper channels.

06

Use Common Sense

Rules cannot cover every situation. Do not deliberately damage the community.
